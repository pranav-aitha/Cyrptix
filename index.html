ZERO-KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE

How Cyrptix Secures Your Secrets

01

PBKDF2 Key Derivation

A 128-bit cryptographic salt is generated via crypto.getRandomValues. Your password undergoes 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 to produce an uncrackable 256-bit symmetric key.

→
02

AES-256-GCM Encryption

A fresh 96-bit Initialization Vector (IV) protects against replay attacks. Plaintext is encrypted with Galois/Counter Mode, guaranteeing both confidential encryption and cryptographic authenticity.

→
03

Bijective Visual Cloaking

The packed payload [Salt + IV + Ciphertext + Tag] is transformed through your chosen cipher module into a 100% reversible visual or invisible stream.
