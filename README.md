## 💎 Why Cyrptix is Different

Most custom ciphers just swap characters around, which is incredibly easy for standard computers to crack. Cyrptix changes the game by combining a **Double-Layer Lock System**:

1. **The Password Gate (AES-256-GCM):** Before any visual magic happens, your message is mathematically locked using a master passphrase. The app runs your password through **600,000 rounds of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 derivation**, generating an uncrackable 256-bit symmetric key. If anyone intercepts your message, they can't force their way in without the exact key.
2. **Bijective Visual Cloaking:** Once securely encrypted, the packed binary block is translated through a specialized codec into one of six highly unique visual layers. To an outsider, your top-secret message looks like a string of emojis, ancient runes, or even a completely blank line.

## 🎛️ Six Specialized Steganographic Modules

Cyrptix features six fully decoupled translation engines that you can swap between seamlessly without losing your current input or passphrase memory:

*   **🔮 Emoji Matrix:** Transforms cryptographic strings into high-density constellations of elemental, cosmic, and mystical emoji characters.
*   **👻 Zero-Width Invisible:** Bit-shifts your data into non-printable, hidden Unicode characters. Your output looks entirely empty on screen, letting you send hidden payloads in plain sight.
*   **ᚠ Pseudo-Script / Runic:** Maps code segments directly into ancient Elder Futhark Norse inscriptions and historical medieval markings.
*   **𓆈 Occult & Esoteric:** Translates values into complex Egyptian hieroglyphs, alchemical marks, musical clefs, and arcane symbols.
*   **𝔄 Aesthetic Fraktur:** Packages your data inside Mathematical Gothic Fraktur layouts and complex Double-Struck typography.
*   **꒰♡꒱ Kaomoji Secret:** Encodes hashes into playful arrays of Japanese ASCII text-face characters, expressive symbols, and emoticons.

## 🛠️ Zero-Knowledge Integrity Architecture

*   **Absolute Data Isolation:** This project operates with zero backend code, tracking trackers, or external database logging. Everything runs entirely within the volatile memory space of your browser sandbox.
*   **Galois/Counter Mode (GCM):** The engine cross-checks every character during parsing. If a third party changes even a single emoji, rune, or invisible spacer during transport, the decryption pipeline throws an absolute cryptographic integrity exception rather than returning corrupted text.
